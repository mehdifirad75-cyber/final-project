YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. This page explains what information is collected, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

  • Account and delivery information: name, email address, phone number, and shipping address when you create an account or place an order.
  • Order information: products purchased and payment confirmation. Full card numbers are handled by the payment provider and are not stored by this store.
  • Usage information: pages visited, searches, and device information used to improve the store.

How information is used

  • To process orders, deliveries, and returns.
  • To send order updates and, where you have agreed, offers and student discounts.
  • To help prevent fraud, maintain security, and improve the website and product selection.

Personal information is not sold to third parties.

Cookies, sharing, and security

Cookies may be used to keep the cart and shopping session working and to understand how the store is used. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; disabling essential cookies may affect site features.

Information is shared with service providers only as needed to process payments, ship orders, or send store emails. Access to personal information is limited to those who need it to provide these services.

Your choices and data retention

  • You can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information by contacting us.
  • You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in the email.
  • Order records may be retained for accounting and warranty purposes.

Policy updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page. If you have questions or a request about your information, please contact us.
