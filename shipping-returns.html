Orders are processed within 24 hours on business days. A tracking link is sent by email when an order ships.

Returns

  • Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
  • Please return items with their original packaging and included accessories.
  • Opened electronics may be refunded based on their condition.
  • Gift cards, downloadable software, and items marked final sale cannot be returned.

How to request a return

  1. Contact us with your order number to start a return.
  2. We will provide return instructions and a prepaid label.
  3. Pack the item securely and send it using the provided label.
  4. After the returned item is inspected, the refund will be issued to the original payment method.

Refunds and damaged items

Refunds are returned to the original payment method after inspection. Processing time may vary by payment provider. Shipping fees are refunded when the return is due to an incorrect or defective item.

If an item arrives damaged or does not work, contact us with your order number and details so we can help.
